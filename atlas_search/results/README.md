# Saved search evidence

Machine outputs from the commands in `../report.md`. Counts always exclude the
fresh identity. The restored learner's bootstrap and query policy are part of
the experimental scope.

- `nine-class-control.sos`, `.hoa`, `nine-class-control.json`: known witness and
  native/stamp plus guarded/closure-only agreement.
- `through-four-selection.json`, `through-four-summary.json`,
  `through-four-results.jsonl`: complete atlas stamp-scope assessment, selected
  one-state residual population and every tested order's outcome.
- `through-four-permutations-selection.json`,
  `through-four-permutations-summary.json`,
  `through-four-permutations-results.jsonl`: exhaustive letter-order search on
  all 32 one-state languages through four positive classes. All 382 distinct
  language/permutation runs are exactly canonical; no limits or failures.
- `five-through-eight-selection.json`, `five-through-eight-summary.json`,
  `five-through-eight-results.jsonl`: observed larger population; stopped at the
  first stall. This is not a completed negative search.
- `five-class.sos`, `five-class-forward.json`, `five-class-closure.json`,
  `five-class-saturated.json`, `five-class-reverse.json`: new witness and its
  exact controls. Forward order stalls; reverse order reaches the canonical
  algebra. Full action tables and counterexamples are retained.
- `five-class-formula.json`: formula, canonical identifier and exact comparison
  outcome from the public formula-construction and stamp-comparison services.
- `five-class-orders.json`: full exact results for all six effective-letter
  orders of the five-class witness. It stalls in precisely three orders, those
  placing `a` before `b`.
- `multiple-strong-selection.json`, `multiple-strong-summary.json`,
  `multiple-strong-results.jsonl`: multi-state single-SCC pilot; stops at the
  three-class cyclic witness after thirteen runs, rather than exhausting its
  larger population.
- `all-through-two-selection.json`, `all-through-two-results.json`: complete
  nine-language scope below three positive classes and all sixteen exact
  canonical ordered outcomes, combining six earlier prefix-independent runs
  with ten new multi-state runs. The aggregation checks full permutation
  coverage and no duplicate outcomes.
- `three-class.sos`, `three-class-orders.json`, `three-class-closure.json`,
  `three-class-saturated.json`, `three-class-formula.json`: minimal witness,
  every letter order, exact-oracle/saturation controls and formula verification.
- `five-class-cyclic-audit.json`, `transient-control.sos`,
  `transient-control.json`: positive and negative boundaries of the post-run
  cyclic distinction audit; the negative input is `a -> Xa`.

The nine-class HOA is copied from the recovered revision's tracked input; SOS
referents are copied from the current atlas. Result files are copied unchanged
from the experiment outputs. Regenerable per-child stdout/stderr remains under
`../tests/logs/`.
